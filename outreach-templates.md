# Cold Outreach Templates — Local Chicago Businesses

## How to use this

Send these **manually** (Gmail, one at a time), not through a bulk tool. For a first wave of 20–30 local businesses, personalized notes from you will out-convert an automated blast by a wide margin — you have zero track record yet, so trust has to be earned one relationship at a time before automating anything.

Fill in every `[bracket]` before sending. If you can't fill in `[specific observation]` with something real and specific about their site, skip that business for this wave — a generic compliment reads as spam regardless of how personal the rest of the email sounds.

Subject lines and body under 100 words. Cold email dies in the second paragraph.

[Your name]

## Notes

- Track who you've contacted and their response in a simple spreadsheet (business name, contact, date sent, follow-up date, status) — the checklist item "Track prospects and replies" from your launch plan.
- Once you get 3-5 real replies/positive signals from a given template, that's your validated message — worth then considering a proper outreach tool (Instantly.ai, Apollo.io, lemlist) to scale it, with a verified custom domain.
- Always give an easy, guilt-free "not interested" — it protects your reputation for the next business you reach out to.
